How pakkeleg works

Everyone brings one to three small wrapped gifts. Number the real packages with stickers so they match the numbers on screen.

Round 1, the pile. Two sets of dice start at opposite sides of the table and travel clockwise, one seat per roll. When you roll a hit, you take a gift from the pile. The round ends when the pile is empty.

Round 2, stealing. The host starts a timer, secret by default. The dice keep moving, but now a hit lets you steal any gift from another player. When time runs out, everyone opens what's in front of them.

What counts as a hit is set by the host: a 6 or doubles (default), any 6, doubles only, or double six only for a long, slow game.

House rules the host can switch on: double 3 in round 2 moves every gift one seat to the left, and last-gift protection means nobody can steal someone's only gift.

If a player doesn't roll in time, the game rolls for them so the dice never get stuck. Everyone plays on their own phone: open this site, type the table code and join. Playing on the same Wi-Fi gives the smoothest connection.
